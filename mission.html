The Burden

A global crisis of sight — and most of it is avoidable.

Aging populations and rising chronic eye disease are outpacing a specialist workforce that cannot scale. The result is a treatable epidemic left unaddressed.

2.2B
Live with vision impairment

Many will never reach a specialist under today's models of care.

1B
Preventable or unaddressed

Nearly half of the burden could have been prevented — or still remains untreated.

What's driving the burden

Aging population

Rising chronic eye disease

Access & workforce gaps

High cost & infrastructure barriers

Transformative Potential

Technology-enabled solutions that extend expertise, simplify care, improve durability, and scale to population need — turning specialist-dependent medicine into care that reaches everyone.

Detect earlier · Restore faster · Treat durably · Reach further
The Strategy

Four integrated pillars, one care continuum.

Each pillar attacks a different failure point — from finding the patient to scaling the cure — and together they close the loop from detection to durable treatment.

01

Find patients & connect to care

AI-enabled screening, clinical decision support, referral tracking, disease monitoring, and precision prognosis.

02

Democratize sight-restoring procedures

Make cataract surgery, corneal restoration, infection treatment, and postoperative care simpler, safer, longer-lasting, and less dependent on infrastructure.

03

Replace burdensome chronic care

Deliver durable therapy for glaucoma, diabetic retinal disease, AMD, myopia, and other progressive conditions to reduce or eliminate repeated visits, injections, and drops.

04

Validate, translate, commercialize & scale

Coordinate clinical validation, regulatory and manufacturing planning, and engagement with foundations, industry, and investors to scale impact.

The Portfolio

Addressing the leading causes of blindness and visual impairment.

Ten-plus technology programs spanning the full care continuum — each pairing a defined global burden with a translational solution built to change the standard of care.

Program 01

Detection, triage & referral

Burden
2.2B people live with vision impairment; many never reach a specialist.
Today's limits
Specialist-dependent exams cannot scale to volume, geography, or chronicity.
Our solution
AI-enabled portable imaging, triage, referral tracking, decision support, and longitudinal monitoring.
The shift
Extends specialist-level decision support beyond the clinic.
Program 02

Myopia & refractive disease

Burden
Myopia will affect 50% of the world by 2050.
Today's limits
Behavior change, atropine, or specialty lenses require long-term adherence.
Our solution
Scalable topical myopia treatment plus AI-enabled screening and monitoring.
The shift
Addresses a population-level epidemic before irreversible complications develop.
Program 03

Cataract

Burden
17M cases of blindness and 83.5M cases of MSVI globally.
Today's limits
Surgery requires capital equipment, training, and cost.
Our solution
Pharmacologic therapy to treat cataract; a low-cost handheld surgical device.
The shift
Makes cataract treatment simpler, cheaper, and more accessible.
Program 04

Corneal blindness

Burden
Corneal infection is a major cause; 12.7M people need a transplant.
Today's limits
Donor scarcity, complex surgery, rejection, and intensive topical dosing.
Our solution
Synthetic tissue and simplified, non-specialist surgery; sustained anti-infective depots.
The shift
Reduces dependence on donor tissue, specialists, and adherence-intensive therapy.
Program 05

Postoperative infection & inflammation

Burden
Tens of millions of eye surgeries; many patients least able to administer drops.
Today's limits
Multi-drop regimens are poorly adhered to, risking infection and inflammation.
Our solution
Drop-free, surgeon-administered, sustained antibiotic and steroid depots.
The shift
Turns postoperative pharmacotherapy from an adherence problem into controlled care.
Program 06

Glaucoma prognosis & treatment

Burden
Affects 80M people; projected 112M by 2040; vision loss is irreversible.
Today's limits
Slow detection; drops need lifelong adherence; surgery is short-lived.
Our solution
AI-enabled prognosis, topical neuroprotection, sustained IOP lowering, and programmable surgery.
The shift
Moves glaucoma from delayed detection to prognosis, durable IOP control, and neuroprotection.
Program 07

Diabetic retinal disease

Burden
Affects 103M adults with diabetes and rising globally.
Today's limits
Anti-VEGF injections require 6–12 per year for years; high cost and adherence burden.
Our solution
A one-time phototherapy implant targeting rod oxygen demand, retinal hypoxia, and VEGF signaling.
The shift
Shifts care from repeated rescue injections to a long-duration implant targeting upstream disease biology.
Program 08

Age-related macular degeneration

Burden
Affects 196M people; projected 288M by 2040; leading cause of central vision loss.
Today's limits
Dry AMD has limited options; wet AMD requires lifelong injections.
Our solution
AI-designed, sustained topical retinal delivery systems.
The shift
Replaces repeated injections with earlier, less invasive, more durable retinal therapy.
